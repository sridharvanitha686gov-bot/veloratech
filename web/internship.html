Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about our training and internship program.

Are the internships remote, hybrid, or in-person?

Our programs are flexible. We support remote collaboration for all interns, with opportunities for local hybrid meetups depending on your location.

Do I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes. Upon successful completion of all core deliverables and your capstone project, you will receive an official Internship Certificate and a letter of recommendation from Velora Tech Solutions.

Is prior coding experience required?

For beginner courses like UI/UX Design, basic computer literacy is enough. For advanced paths like AI or SaaS architecture, basic programming foundations (Python/JS) are highly recommended.

How does the selection process work?

Once you submit your application through the contact form, our team reviews your profile and background. Selected applicants are scheduled for a brief technical discussion to align you with a suitable cohort.
